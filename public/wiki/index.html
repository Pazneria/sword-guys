01

First steps

A new game starts at level 1 in Greenhollow with 55 gold, starter equipment, three Small Potions, an Antidote, and two spells: Spark and Mend. HP is Ari's health; MP is spent on spells. Your goal is to finish the Five Roads story. There is no score or leaderboard.

  1. Talk to Elder Rowan. He is north of your starting position. Stand next to him, face him, and tap OK or press E, Enter, or Space. Use the same button or key to advance the dialogue.
  2. Check your map. Tap Map or press N to see your area and next objective. The shop is in Greenhollow's northwest house, the inn is in the northeast house, and the exit is at the south edge.
  3. Recover the Cave Relic. Leave town and head northeast to Mossvale Cave. Travel through the Cave Mouth and Glowcap Split to the Relic Grotto, then interact with the relic chest.
  4. Return to Rowan. He opens the Waymeet route and gives you 70 gold and an Iron Sword. Open the Equipment tab and equip the sword; receiving an upgrade does not replace your equipped weapon.
  5. Prepare for the next road. Heal with Mend or a potion, or rest at the inn to recover both HP and MP. Save before leaving, then follow the map objective to the Wounded Courier and Broker Sel.

Rowan calls Mossvale the "west cave," but it is northeast of Greenhollow on the map. An optional chest in Glowcap Split contains a Leather Cap, which you can equip from the menu.

02

Controls

The game supports a keyboard and on-screen touch controls. The Pause button is available on desktop and mobile.

Touch movement and interaction

Hold one of the eight arrows to move, or slide between arrows to change direction. The corner arrows move diagonally; a short tap turns Ari without walking. Hold movement with one finger and press OK with another to interact. Each finger works independently.

OK interacts or confirms, and Back cancels. These buttons act once per press. Dialogue also has Next / OK and Close buttons; rest, sleep, and save prompts have Confirm and Cancel.

Touch menus and map

Tap Menu, then a tab and an entry. Swipe the tab strip to reach later tabs and scroll long lists. Tapping a usable item uses it immediately; tapping equipment equips it, and tapping a healing spell casts it. You can also select with the arrows and act with OK. Back, Close menu, or Menu closes the menu.

Tap Map to open the area map. Map, OK, Back, or Close map closes it. Menu and Map are unavailable during battle; battle commands have their own tappable choices.

With a keyboard, the direction keys also select menu entries and battle choices.

Keyboard menus and shops

Left and right change menu tabs; up and down select an entry. Confirm acts on the selection, and cancel closes the menu. In shops, left and right switch between Buy and Sell. Close the map with N, confirm, or cancel.

Face a person or object

Tap a direction to turn toward what you want to interact with, then use OK or a confirm key when the prompt appears. Holding a direction starts walking after about 0.1 seconds. Shopkeepers and innkeepers can be reached across their counters.

Pause, restart, and exit

Pause stops play and animations. Choose Resume to continue where you left off. Switching away from the page also pauses the game; choose Resume when you return.

Restart checkpoint asks for confirmation before returning to the last checkpoint held in the current session. It ends any battle and discards progress made since that checkpoint. Cancel returns to Pause. Restarting leaves the browser's saved slot unchanged.

Exit game opens an exit screen and keeps your session available through Return to game. It neither closes the browser nor saves. Use Menu → Save before closing or reloading to keep recent progress.

Touch controls support portrait and landscape. Rotation or interrupted touches release held controls. See testing limits for device coverage.

03

Exploration

The map's Next label names your objective and its destination. Use the legend to find Ari, goals, exits, enemies, shops, and save services. The map shows your current area; travel by walking to exits, not by clicking a destination.

Roads and dungeon rooms

Walk onto an entrance, exit, or staircase to change areas. Locked routes tell you what remains to do. Dungeons have several rooms, with optional branches containing treasure. Each chest opens once per journey.

Encounters and safe stops

Walking through encounter zones can start random battles, even without a visible enemy. Contact with a visible enemy also starts a battle, and some enemies chase Ari. Villages are safe. Dialogue, menus, shops, and the map pause exploration and enemy movement.

If the objective points to an earlier road, speak to the person it names. These conversations advance the objective text even when you have already unlocked the next route.

04

Combat

Battles wait for your command. Ari acts first, then surviving enemies respond in order of their Speed, until Ari falls or they have all acted. Defeating an enemy before its response prevents its attack that turn.

Attack
Select an enemy that is still alive. Damage depends on Ari's Attack and the enemy's Defense.
Magic
Choose a learned spell, then a target if it deals damage. Healing spells act on Ari. Casting costs MP; trying to cast an unavailable spell or one you cannot afford does not spend a turn.
Item
Choose a usable inventory item. It consumes one item and a turn, even if Ari's HP or MP is already full.
Defend
Reduces damage during the enemies' next response without healing Ari. It halves base physical damage and reduces base magic damage to 65%, before a small random addition.
Run
Each attempt has a 72% chance of escaping a normal battle. A failed attempt lets enemies respond. Escape is forbidden in the four story boss fights.

On touch, tap a command, then any spell or item it asks you to choose. Attack and damage spells open a target list; tap an enemy's name there to act. Defend and Run act as soon as you tap them. With a keyboard, select using the direction keys and confirm. Back or a cancel key leaves a submenu; at the main command screen it selects Run when allowed, but does not attempt to escape.

Choose Continue / OK after victory or escape to return to exploration. After defeat, the same button restores your checkpoint. Wait for battle animations to finish before making another choice; presses during animations are ignored.

Item and spell limits

Revive Charm heals a living Ari for 80 HP; it does not revive him after defeat. Spell elements have no weakness or resistance bonuses. Status effects are not active, so Antidote has no effect when used.

05

Equipment and spells

Winning battles earns experience (XP), gold, and a chance of consumable drops. Story bosses also grant quest rewards and open routes. Leveling up raises Ari's maximum HP, maximum MP, Attack, Defense, and Magic; Speed rises on even levels. Check Menu → Status for your stats and XP toward the next level.

Equip an upgrade

Ari has slots for a weapon, helmet, body armor, leg armor, shield, and two accessories. In Menu → Equipment, select an owned item and confirm. Equipment changes your stats; some heavy armor lowers Speed. There is no crafting or equipment upgrade system.

Purchased equipment is equipped automatically only if its default slot is empty. To replace starter equipment, equip the new item yourself. Accessories fill the first slot if empty, otherwise the second. There is no slot picker or Unequip command.

Shops and inns

Shops sell items for gold and buy them back for 45% of their base price, rounded down. Key items cannot be sold. To sell equipped gear, replace it with another owned item first.

An inn stay restores all HP and MP, takes Ari to a room, and saves a checkpoint. It costs 8g in Greenhollow, 18g in Waymeet, 28g in Lumaire, 42g in Ironmarch, or 64g in Sunspire. Confirm the stay with the innkeeper. Using the room's bed also heals and saves.

06

Five Roads walkthrough

The story leads from Greenhollow through Waymeet, Lumaire, Ironmarch, and Sunspire to Eclipse Tower. Expand a route for its objectives and rewards. The walkthrough contains story and progression spoilers.

1 Greenhollow and Mossvale Cave

Speak to Elder Rowan, recover the Cave Relic from its chest in Mossvale Relic Grotto, then return to Rowan. He gives you 70g and an Iron Sword and opens the Waymeet route. Mossvale has three rooms and no story boss.

The optional chest in Glowcap Split contains a Leather Cap. On the next road, speak to the Wounded Courier.

2 Waymeet and Dustbridge Ruins

Speak to Broker Sel, then travel through Dustbridge Ruins and Ledger Hall to the Map Room. Defeat Bandit Captain Rusk to receive the Road Seal, a 120g quest reward in addition to battle rewards, and access to Lumaire.

The optional Broken Loop branch contains a Copper Ring. Speak to the Stranded Ferryman on the road to Lumaire.

3 Lumaire and the Flooded Shrine

Oracle Niva starts the shrine quest and teaches Ember. Travel through the Flooded Shrine and Reed Maze to the Lumen Sanctum. Defeat the Mire Warden to receive the Shrine Lumen, a 180g quest reward, River Mend, and access to Ironmarch.

The optional Sluice Chapel branch contains a River Charm. Speak to the Ash-Covered Miner on the road to Ironmarch.

4 Ironmarch and Ironvein Fortress

Speak to Marshal Brinn, then travel through Ironvein Fortress and the Barracks to the Castellan Chamber. Defeat the Iron Castellan to receive the Iron Writ, a 240g quest reward, and access to Sunspire.

The optional Forge Core branch contains an Ember Amulet. Speak to the Bell Runner on the road to Sunspire.

5 Sunspire and Eclipse Tower

Keeper Aster gives you the Eclipse Key and opens the final route. Meet Aster's Page on the road, then travel through Eclipse Tower and its Library to the Observatory. Defeat the Hollow Regent to reach the ending. The optional Shadow Stair branch contains a Sunward Aegis.

Continue Exploring keeps your progress. Start Over begins a new journey and replaces your saved slot. Unlike Restart checkpoint in Pause, Start Over discards the previous journey.

Defeating Rusk, the Mire Warden, or the Iron Castellan completes that quest and opens the next road immediately. You do not need to return to the quest giver. Road conversations update your objective text but do not control these unlocks.

07

Saves and defeat

The game keeps a checkpoint in the current session and one saved slot in this browser. Menu → Save, a confirmed Save Crystal interaction, an inn stay, or sleeping in an inn bed updates both with your current position, stats, inventory, equipment, spells, and quest and map progress. The session checkpoint is what defeat and Restart checkpoint restore. Winning a battle or completing a quest does not save automatically.

Resume your saved journey

Opening the game loads a valid save without changing the saved data. A new starting save is created only when the slot is empty. If saved data is malformed, unsupported, or unreadable, it is retained and you can still play from Greenhollow. Saving, resting, sleeping, or choosing Start Over at the ending can replace that stored data.

Load or recover from defeat

Menu → Load replaces your current progress with the browser's saved checkpoint. After defeat, confirm to restore the last checkpoint held in the current session. XP, gold, items, and quest progress earned since that checkpoint are lost. There is no additional gold penalty.

Browser storage

Saves stay in this browser's site storage, without an account or cloud sync. There is no save export, import, or cross-device transfer. If storage is blocked, checkpoints work only until you close or reload the page. The game still displays a save message even when it cannot store the save.

Restart checkpoint and Exit leave the saved slot unchanged. Restart restores the session's checkpoint; Exit keeps the session available through Return to game. Neither saves recent changes. After closing or reloading, you resume the last valid browser save.

After choosing Continue Exploring at the ending, use Menu → Save to keep that progress for next time. Reading this guide does not affect your save.

08

Settings and limits

  • Game modes: the Five Roads campaign continues into exploration after the ending. There is no multiplayer, alternate campaign, score mode, or difficulty selector.
  • Options: this tab lists controls. It has no volume, key rebinding, graphics, or gameplay settings.
  • Enemy attacks: each enemy uses one fixed action. Alternate boss attacks listed in the source are not used.
  • Enemy reappearance: ordinary visible enemies return when their map reloads. Defeated story bosses stay removed unless you load an earlier checkpoint. Random encounters continue.
  • Development shortcuts: F9 and F10 grant playtest stats or unlock routes in development builds. They are unavailable in the published game.
  • Device testing: touch gameplay, portrait and landscape layouts, and save preservation were checked in Chromium phone emulation. Physical phones, Safari/WebKit, real device cutouts and browser controls, and sustained performance have not been tested.
