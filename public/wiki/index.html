Before you begin: checkpoints have a reload limitation.

In the reviewed build, opening or reloading the game replaces the local save slot with a fresh starting checkpoint. Keep your existing game tab open while reading. How saves work →

01

First steps

You begin at level 1 in Greenhollow with 55 gold, starter equipment, three Small Potions, an Antidote, and the spells Spark and Mend. The goal is to finish the Five Roads story; there is no score or leaderboard.

  1. Talk to Elder Rowan. He is north of your starting position. Stand next to him, face him, and press E, Enter, or Space. Confirm advances each dialogue line.
  2. Check your map. Press N to see your current area, the next objective, and its destination. In Greenhollow, the shop is at the northwest house and the inn at the northeast house; the exit is at the south edge.
  3. Recover the Cave Relic. Leave town and head to Mossvale Cave, northeast of Greenhollow on the current overworld map (tile 23, 10). Travel through the Cave Mouth and Glowcap Split to the Relic Grotto. Interact with the relic chest.
  4. Return to Rowan. He opens the Waymeet route and gives you 70 gold and an Iron Sword. Open the Equipment tab and equip the sword; receiving an upgrade does not replace your equipped weapon.
  5. Prepare for the next road. Heal with Mend or a potion, rest at the inn when needed, and make a checkpoint before leaving. Follow the map objective to the Wounded Courier and then Broker Sel.

Two useful details: Rowan calls it the “west cave,” but the current map places it northeast of Greenhollow. The optional chest in Mossvale Glowcap Split contains a Leather Cap; equip it from the menu.

Menus and shops

Left/right changes menu tabs; up/down selects a row; confirm acts; cancel closes. In a shop, left/right switches Buy and Sell. On the map, N, confirm, or cancel closes it.

Facing and interaction

Movement supports eight directions. From idle, a held direction turns Ari first, then moves after about 0.1 seconds. Face the person or object and look for the interaction prompt. Shop and inn counters allow talking across them.

Reading on mobile

This guide supports touch scrolling and tap navigation. The reviewed game source uses keyboard input and has no touch movement pad or touch battle controls. Any later mobile layout needs a new source check before its controls are added here.

03

Finding your way

The map is your main navigation aid. Its “Next” text names the current objective and, when needed, another destination map. The legend identifies you, goals, exits, enemies, and shop/save services. It shows the current area rather than a complete clickable world-travel screen.

Roads and rooms

Walk onto an entrance, exit, or stair transition to move between areas. Locked routes show a message naming what remains to do. Dungeons have several rooms and optional treasure branches. Chests open once in the current progress state.

Encounters and safe stops

Moving through encounter zones can trigger random battles even when no enemy is visible. Touching a visible enemy also starts a battle; aggressive enemies may chase Ari. Village areas are safe. Opening dialogue, a menu, a shop, or the map pauses exploration and enemy movement.

If the objective seems behind you: speak to the road NPC it names. Road conversations update the objective even though the boss or key route gate may already be open.

04

Combat, one turn at a time

Ari acts first. After a valid action, every surviving enemy takes an action, ordered by enemy speed. Battles wait for your command, so take time to choose. Defeating one target before its response removes its attack from that round.

Attack
Choose a living target and confirm. Physical damage uses Ari’s Attack against that enemy’s Defense.
Magic
Select a learned spell. Damage spells then ask for a target; healing spells restore Ari’s HP. Casting costs MP. A missing spell or insufficient MP does not consume a turn.
Item
Select a usable inventory item. Using it consumes one item and a turn, including when your HP or MP is already full.
Defend
Reduces damage for the immediate enemy response. The physical damage formula halves its base damage before the small random addition; Defend does not heal Ari.
Run
Normal battles allow a 72% escape attempt. Failure gives enemies their response. The four story boss fights forbid escape. Cancel backs out of a submenu; at the main command screen it selects Run when escape is allowed, without attempting it.

Use direction keys to choose commands, then up/down to select spells, items, or targets. Confirm the victory, escape, or defeat result to leave the battle.

Read the HP bar, not just the item name.

The Revive Charm restores 80 HP to a living Ari; it is not an automatic resurrection. Element names and descriptions do not currently apply weakness or resistance multipliers. Antidotes have a cure-status definition, but the reviewed battle simulation does not apply or cure statuses.

05

Gear, gold & magic

Winning a battle grants XP and gold from its enemies, plus a chance of consumable drops. Story boss victories also grant quest rewards and route unlocks. XP levels Ari up, raising maximum HP, maximum MP, Attack, Defense, and Magic; Speed rises on even levels. Status shows the current stats and XP toward the next level.

Equip your upgrades

Ari has a weapon, helmet, body armor, leg armor, shield, and two accessory slots. Open Menu → Equipment, select an owned item, and confirm. Gear modifies the listed stats; some heavy pieces lower Speed. There is no crafting or upgrade system in this build.

Buying gear only auto-equips an empty default slot. Replace starter equipment manually. Accessory selection fills the first accessory slot if empty, otherwise the second; the menu has no explicit slot picker or Unequip command.

Spend and recover

Shops buy and sell items for gold. Sell values are 45% of purchase price, rounded down. Key items cannot be sold. An item currently equipped cannot be sold; replace it with another owned item first.

Inn stays fully restore HP and MP and save a checkpoint. Prices are Greenhollow 8g, Waymeet 18g, Lumaire 28g, Ironmarch 42g, and Sunspire 64g. Confirm with the innkeeper; the stay takes you to the room. Interacting with its bed also heals and saves.

06

The Five Roads

The campaign follows Greenhollow → Waymeet → Lumaire → Ironmarch → Sunspire → Eclipse Tower. Open the route notes below for objectives, boss destinations, and rewards. These notes contain progression spoilers.

1 Greenhollow & Mossvale Cave

Speak to Elder Rowan, open the Cave Relic chest in Mossvale Relic Grotto, then return to Rowan. He grants 70g and an Iron Sword and opens the Waymeet route. Mossvale has three rooms and no story boss.

Treasure branch: Leather Cap in Glowcap Split. Next road conversation: Wounded Courier.

2 Waymeet & Dustbridge Ruins

Speak to Broker Sel. Travel through Dustbridge Ruins and Ledger Hall to the Map Room; defeat Bandit Captain Rusk. Victory grants the Road Seal, completes Road Trouble, adds its 120g quest reward to battle rewards, and opens Lumaire automatically.

Treasure branch: Copper Ring in the Broken Loop. Next road conversation: Stranded Ferryman.

3 Lumaire & the Flooded Shrine

Speak to Oracle Niva to start the shrine quest and learn Ember. Travel through the Flooded Shrine and Reed Maze to the Lumen Sanctum; defeat the Mire Warden. Victory grants the Shrine Lumen, a 180g quest reward, River Mend, and access to Ironmarch.

Treasure branch: River Charm in the Sluice Chapel. Next road conversation: Ash-Covered Miner.

4 Ironmarch & Ironvein Fortress

Speak to Marshal Brinn. Travel through Ironvein Fortress and its Barracks to the Castellan Chamber; defeat the Iron Castellan. Victory grants the Iron Writ, a 240g quest reward, and access to Sunspire.

Treasure branch: Ember Amulet in the Forge Core. Next road conversation: Bell Runner.

5 Sunspire & Eclipse Tower

Speak to Keeper Aster for the Eclipse Key and the final route unlock. Meet Aster’s Page on the road. Travel through Eclipse Tower and its Library to the Observatory; defeat the Hollow Regent to reach the ending.

Treasure branch: Sunward Aegis in the Shadow Stair. The ending offers Continue Exploring, which keeps your progress, and Start Over, which creates a new journey and replaces the checkpoint.

No extra hand-in after the middle bosses: the Rusk, Mire Warden, and Iron Castellan victories complete their quests and unlock the next roads immediately. Their quest gold is separate from enemy gold. Road NPC conversations guide the objective list but do not gate those unlocks.

07

Saves & defeat

There is one local checkpoint slot, with no account or cloud sync. Menu → Save, a confirmed Save Crystal interaction, an inn stay, and sleeping in an inn bed all replace it. A checkpoint stores your position, stats, inventory, equipment, spells, quest progress, and map progress. Walking, winning a fight, or completing a quest does not itself save.

Reload currently replaces the save

The reviewed game starts a new state and immediately writes a starting checkpoint to browser storage. It does not automatically load an earlier save. Reloading or opening a second game tab can therefore overwrite that slot before you can choose Load. Keep the active game tab open; treat this build’s saves as checkpoints within a session.

Loading and defeat

Menu → Load reads the local slot and replaces your current progress with it. After defeat, confirm to restore the last in-memory checkpoint. You lose changes made after that checkpoint, including newly earned XP, gold, items, and quest progress. There is no separate gold penalty.

Storage and scope

If browser storage is blocked, the game still keeps an in-memory checkpoint, but it cannot persist it. The save message does not distinguish a blocked storage write. Saves belong to the browser’s site storage; there is no export/import, multiple slots, or cross-device transfer UI.

The wiki never touches your save. It has no scripts or browser-storage access. Read it in a separate tab without reloading the game. After continuing past the ending, use Menu → Save if you want a checkpoint of that progress.

08

Settings & current limits

  • One campaign. The available play is the Five Roads story and continued exploration after its ending. There is no multiplayer, alternate campaign, score mode, or difficulty selector.
  • Options is a control reference. It does not offer volume sliders, key rebinding, graphics presets, or gameplay settings.
  • Combat has a limited ruleset. Spell elements currently label visuals, with no resistance system. Status effects are not applied or cured. Enemies use their first defined action; weighted alternate boss moves are not selected.
  • Enemy reappearance. Ordinary visible enemies return when their map reloads. Defeated story bosses stay removed in the saved progress state. Random encounter zones still produce battles.
  • Development tools are separate. F9 grants playtest stats and F10 grants stats plus route unlocks only in a development build. They are not available in the published production build.
  • Planned work is not a player feature. Developer goals include further art and polish; the goal document is not a guarantee of implemented mechanics. This guide does not claim completion of those goals.
