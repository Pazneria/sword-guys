# Sword Guys - Controls & Features

## Controls

### Movement
- **Arrow Keys** or **WASD** - Move player in 4 directions
- Player faces the direction they're moving

### Actions
- **SPACE** or **Z** or **ENTER** - Interact with NPCs, advance dialogue
- **M** - Open/Close menu (Status, Inventory, Equipment)
- **ESC** - Close menus, go back

### In Battle
- **Arrow Up/Down** - Select action/item
- **SPACE/Z/ENTER** - Confirm selection
- **ESC/X** - Go back (in item menu)

## Game Features

### Exploration
- **Multiple Maps**: Overworld, Town, Cave
- **Map Transitions**: Walk to certain tiles to travel between maps
- **Random Encounters**: Walking in certain areas triggers battles
- **NPCs**: Talk to characters for dialogue

### Combat System
- **Turn-based battles** with various enemies
- **Actions**: Attack, Use Items, Flee
- **Experience & Leveling**: Gain EXP and level up after victories
- **Loot Drops**: Enemies drop gold and items
- **Status Tracking**: HP/MP management during battle

### Inventory & Equipment
- **Inventory System**: Collect and use items
- **Equipment Slots**: Weapon, Armor, Accessory
- **Menu System**: Access via 'M' key
  - Status Tab: View character stats
  - Items Tab: View and use items
  - Equipment Tab: View equipped gear

### NPCs & Dialogue
- **Interact** with NPCs by facing them and pressing SPACE
- **Shopkeepers**: Some NPCs have shops (indicated by '$' symbol)
- **Multiple dialogue lines**: NPCs can have conversations

### Progression
- **Leveling System**: Gain experience from battles
- **Stat Growth**: HP, MP, Strength, Defense, Agility, Intelligence all increase on level up
- **Gold Economy**: Buy and sell items at shops
- **Full heal on level up**

## Tips
- Save your game regularly (feature to be added)
- Talk to all NPCs for hints and information
- Explore different areas to find items and enemies
- Manage your resources - potions and ethers are valuable!
- Higher encounter rates in caves/dungeons

## Enemy Types
- **Slime** - Weak basic enemy (Overworld)
- **Goblin** - Common enemy (Overworld, Cave)
- **Wolf** - Fast and aggressive (Overworld)
- **Bat** - Drains life (Cave)
- **Skeleton** - Tough undead warrior (Cave)
- **Dragon** - Powerful boss enemy

## Items
### Consumables
- **Potion** - Restores 50 HP
- **Hi-Potion** - Restores 150 HP
- **Ether** - Restores 30 MP
- **Antidote** - Cures poison
- **Revive** - Revives fallen ally (50% HP)

### Equipment
- **Weapons**: Wooden Sword, Iron Sword, Steel Sword, Excalibur, Staff
- **Armor**: Cloth Armor, Leather Armor, Iron Shield, Steel Armor
- **Accessories**: Power Ring, Speed Boots, Wisdom Amulet
