Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: Sword Guys RPG environment tileset, final production art
Primary request: Create a transparent-background 32x32 tile pixel-art environment tileset for Eclipse Tower.
Scene/backdrop: Starless black-stone tower interior with violet magical light, archive shelves, observatory gate pieces, rune-carved stone, cracked shadow floors, and late-game dungeon props.
Style/medium: Clean readable pixel art for a compact top-down RPG, orthographic tile view, crisp edges, hand-painted fantasy tiles.
Composition/framing: One 320x192 PNG tilesheet arranged as a clear 10-column by 6-row grid of 32x32 tiles. Each cell must read as an individual reusable tile or prop. Include seamless walkable floor variants, cracked floor variants, wall tops, wall bases, dark trim, stair/threshold pieces, archive shelf props, violet crystal lamps, observatory gate pieces, window/arch pieces, and a few blocking props with collision-friendly silhouettes.
Lighting/mood: Dark tower mood with controlled violet glow and readable value separation between walkable floors and blocking walls/props.
Color palette: Charcoal, near-black stone, muted blue-gray, violet highlights, small silver/gold occult accents.
Transparency plan: Put unused transparent regions and freestanding prop backgrounds on a perfectly flat solid #00ff00 chroma-key background for local removal. Do not use #00ff00 anywhere in the actual tiles.
Constraints: Tile boundaries must align to the 32x32 grid. Keep every tile top-down or orthographic, not a perspective scene. Walkable ground and blocking props must remain visually distinct at game scale. No characters, monsters, UI, labels, letters, text, watermark, signatures, cast shadows, or scenic camera composition.
Avoid: Recycled art, other-project-looking assets, isometric perspective, rendered 3D, giant single illustration, uneven tile cells, visible text, or decorative borders outside the tile grid.
