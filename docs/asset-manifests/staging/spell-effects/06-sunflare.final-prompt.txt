Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: Sword Guys battle spell animation strip, final production art
Primary request: Create a dramatic short pixel-art spell effect strip for Sunflare.
Subject: Golden late-game light burst with a piercing sunbeam, expanding halo ring, radiant star impact, and lingering gold motes.
Style/medium: Clean readable compact RPG pixel art, heroic solar magic, no character art.
Composition/framing: One horizontal row of 12 evenly spaced animation frames, each frame a centered 64x64 square. Start with a bright golden point, open into a thick sunbeam flare, bloom into a large star-shaped radiant impact that uses most of the 64x64 frame without touching the edges, then fade into a halo ring and gold motes. The final strip will be normalized to 768x64.
Lighting/mood: Powerful final-route light spell, explosive and heroic, bright but still readable over a battle backdrop.
Color palette: Rich gold, warm yellow, white-hot center, orange rim highlights, tiny amber spark motes.
Transparency plan: Put the entire strip on a perfectly flat solid #ff00ff chroma-key background for local removal. Do not use #ff00ff anywhere in the spell effect.
Constraints: No frame labels, no text, no numbers, no character, no scenery, no UI, no watermark. Frames must stay evenly spaced in one row with no decorative borders. The biggest impact should be dramatic inside its 64x64 frame, not a full-screen explosion.
Avoid: Sun landscape, sky scene, black background, letters, character hands, tiny weak spark, flat line-only beam, oversized full-screen explosion, or ornate border.
